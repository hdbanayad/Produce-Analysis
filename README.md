# Project Background

A Chinese supermarket sells produce across six product categories comprising 166 unique family SKUs.

The company possesses a large volume of sales, sales velocity, and operational data that has historically been underutilized. This project thoroughly analyzes and synthesizes that data to uncover actionable insights aimed at improving the grocery store’s overall performance and decision-making.

Insights and recommendations are provided in the following areas:

- **Sales Trends Analysis**: Evaluation of historical sales patterns by product, with a focus on revenue and order volume.
- **Product-Level Performance**: Analysis of individual SKUs to understand their contribution to total sales and overall performance.
- **Category Comparison**: Comparison of sales and order volume across product categories to identify high- and low-performing segments.
